# Distribution

## Stars are downstream of audience, not quality

The most-starred free Flutter admin panel on GitHub has 7,110 stars and contains **one screen** — a
dashboard view and a sidebar. FlareLine, with 22 screens, auth, charts, tables, forms and ten
locales, has 158.

The difference is not the code. The author of the 7,110-star repo is a Google Developer Expert who
runs a Flutter YouTube channel and has 13,298 GitHub followers; his other repos (4,834★, 2,112★,
1,837★, 1,532★) are all UI-only tutorial companions.

**Roughly 45:1 in favour of audience over completeness.** That variable cannot be out-built. It can
only be out-distributed.

AdminLTE alone is five times the entire free Flutter admin field. One README line there reaches
more developers than any launch post, and it is the one channel no competitor can replicate.

## Channels, in order of leverage

1. **Cross-link from AdminLTE and gentelella.** Costs nothing. Low conversion on a very large
   number still beats everything below it. Note the audience is Bootstrap/PHP developers, not
   Flutter developers — expect the rate to look bad in isolation and judge on absolute numbers.
2. **This README's format.** See the comment block at the top of README.md. Do not rearrange it.
3. **GitHub metadata.** Keyword-loaded repo name, not brand-loaded. Topics: `flutter`,
   `flutter-web`, `admin-dashboard`, `admin-panel`, `dashboard-template`, `flutter-admin`, `dart`.
4. **Say "dashboard", not "admin panel".** `flutter dashboard` is the highest-impression query in
   Search Console and the word the incumbent uses throughout his prose.
5. **pub.dev.** Phase 2 — publishing `kite_ui` earns a package listing, a scoring badge and a
   permanent link back. A discovery channel no competitor uses.
6. **Directories.** awesome-flutter, Flutter Gems, itsallwidgets.com, flutterlibrary.com, and the
   medevel / daily.dev roundups that already rank for these queries.
7. **Video.** No Flutter YouTube presence in-house, and video is the one method demonstrably
   working here. One build-along — made or sponsored — is likely worth more than three extra
   dashboards.

## Positioning, precisely

Not "run your admin panel on a phone" — admin panels are desk tools and that is a solution looking
for a problem. The claim is **"the admin panel for your Flutter app"**: same models, same API
client, same language, same team. Cross-platform is the proof, not the pitch.

## Gate on use, not applause

A star costs nothing and mostly reports who saw the link. Downloads and clones report who ran the
thing. **Pro edition gate: 2,000 pub.dev downloads/month or 150 clones/week at 90 days.**
Thresholds are estimates — replace them once a week-one baseline exists.
